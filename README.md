n the digital age, the rapid spread of misinformation and fake news has become a major concern, influencing public opinion, undermining trust in media, and even affecting elections and public health. With the overwhelming volume of information shared on social media and news platforms, manual fact-checking is neither scalable nor timely. Therefore, automating fake news detection using Natural Language Processing (NLP) has become an essential task in the field of Artificial Intelligence.

This project aims to develop a robust fake news detection system using Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers (BERT)—a state-of-the-art transformer-based model developed by Google. BERT's deep contextual understanding of language makes it especially effective for tasks involving semantics and language nuances, which are critical in distinguishing between real and fake news.

The system processes text-based news articles or social media posts and classifies them as real or fake. The project involves preprocessing text data, fine-tuning the BERT model on a labeled dataset, and evaluating the model's performance using metrics such as accuracy. By leveraging transfer learning and pre-trained language models, this approach seeks to achieve high accuracy and generalizability in fake news detection across various topics and sources.
